Help

Getting recommendations

  1. Register (Student, Business buyer or Consumer) and log in.
  2. Fill in the scenario form: budget, preferred OS, storage and your role-specific details.
  3. Open a result to compare Local price with the Dubai landed cost (price + freight + duty + VAT).
  4. Use Save to keep a laptop on your wishlist, Save scenario to revisit it, or Export PDF.
